# CEFR Level Calibration: German B1 (Zertifikat Deutsch)

### B1 LINGUISTIC CALIBRATION & EXPECTATIONS
- **Target Partner Complexity:** Natural, everyday B1 German (<= 22 words per sentence).
- **Expected Candidate Grammar at B1:**
  * Main clauses with verb in 2nd position and inversion after fronted adverbs/time expressions (`Meiner Meinung nach ist...`, `Am Samstag habe ich...`, `Deshalb...`, `Trotzdem...`).
  * Subordinate clauses (`weil`, `dass`, `obwohl`, `wenn`, `falls`) with the conjugated verb at the very end.
  * `seit` + Dative with Present tense for ongoing actions (`Ich lerne seit acht Monaten Deutsch`), `als` before professions (`Ich arbeite als...`), and country prepositions (`aus der Türkei`, `aus dem Iran`).
  * Modal verbs (`können`, `müssen`, `sollen`, `wollen`, `möchten`) and polite *Konjunktiv II* requests/proposals (`könnten`, `würden`, `wäre`).
- **B1 Error Tolerance:** Minor B1 grammatical slips that do NOT block comprehension still earn Grade **[B]**!
